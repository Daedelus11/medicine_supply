What We Do

Commercial, institutional, and select residential architecture
across Idaho, Washington, Oregon, Hawaii, and Kentucky.

From ground-up commercial construction to tenant improvements, developer-led multifamily, and institutional projects. Fine Line Studio covers the full project lifecycle across all project types and delivery methods.

Commercial Architecture

Offices, financial institutions, mixed-use, and tenant improvements. Delivered on schedule, within budget, with direct principal involvement at every phase.

Institutional Projects

Schools, civic buildings, military facilities, and faith-based projects. Experience navigating public agency requirements and design review.

Residential Architecture

Select custom residential. The same discipline, the same delivery.

Developer & Multifamily

Townhomes, infill residential, and prototype buildings for multi-location rollout. Fast delivery, value-engineered without sacrificing design.

Industrial & Warehouse

Distribution, manufacturing, and aviation facilities. Functional design with attention to workflow, code compliance, and future adaptability.

Permitting & Construction Administration

Full construction document packages, agency submittals, RFI responses, and on-site oversight. Projects delivered the way they were designed.

The Principal

Mark Charles Wagner,
AIA, NCARB

Mark Wagner has been practicing architecture for over thirty years, delivering more than 300 projects across commercial, institutional, civic, and residential sectors, with a combined construction value exceeding $250 million.

Fine Line Studio is a principal-led practice. When you hire Fine Line, you work directly with Mark from the first conversation through construction completion. No hand-offs. No junior staff managing your project. Every drawing, every site visit, every decision carries his direct attention.
